# Code of Conduct

We want OpenSong to be a welcoming project for everyone who wants to help, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.
- Credit other people's work.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, doxxing or threats.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing discussions, or spam.
- Sharing someone's private information without permission.
- Encouraging or sharing copyright infringement, DRM circumvention or scraping of services against their terms in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, issues and contributions that break this code, and may restrict participation of anyone who does. To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the GitHub profile contact options or a private security advisory; reports are handled in confidence.

This code is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1 (<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>).
